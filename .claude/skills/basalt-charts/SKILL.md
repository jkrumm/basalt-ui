name: basalt-charts
description: Add or extend a chart in a basalt-ui app — pick a shipped kind or compose CartesianChart, register series colors, verify.
when_to_use: User wants to add or restyle a chart, introduce a metric and its color, or extract a repeated chart shape. Companion to /basalt-design.
---

Procedure only; the contract is `.claude/rules/basalt-charts.md`.

## Steps

1. Write the one-sentence question the chart answers, then the surface it sits in: the parts it needs (the mark, the figure, the detail list, table or timeline), where each sits and what they share. A chart with a detail list sits beside it at a wide container, the list scrolling in its own region at the chart's height, and stacks at a narrow one, except a form laid out along time or a chain (`IntervalTrack`, a timeline, `ChainView`), which takes the row's full width with its list below (n35's week track in a half-width card cut nine titles); the footprint fits the question. Then pick the form that answers it (the reader's next action decides: a running stage, a median against p95, a pair of rows), then the cheapest substrate that draws that form: a shipped kind whose form is that form, else `CartesianChart` marks in the page's own chart file. A kind is never chosen because it is the nearest catalogue row, and a missing kind is never a reason to draw a different form. A bespoke form stays in that chart file; one repeated in this app is extracted there, never into a new `basalt-ui/charts` kind. Shipped kinds by form (each in `node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/<kebab-name>.md`): `Lines` or `Bars` for a value over time or a category; `Sparkline` for a trend in a cell or tile; `BandStrip` for one gap-free row of states over time; `Heatmap` for two categories crossed, the cell value the answer; `IntervalTrack` for spans and point events on rows over time (a calendar week, the steps of a run, outages by day); else `CartesianChart` marks.
2. Register each new series color in the app's one series file (the one file that writes colour literals): `groupTokens('health', defineSeries({ hrv: { light, dark } }))` from `basalt-ui/tokens` (lighter on dark, deeper on light), then pass the group as `<BasaltProvider paletteOptions={{ groups: [SERIES] }} />` (or `buildPaletteCss` for SSR). An HTML mark (a `Meter`, a track `div`, a dot) takes the same colours as `var(--basalt-…)` refs from `seriesTokens` or `groupTokens`, so kinds keep one colour in the chart, the list and the track, never close greys (n35, n38, n33). A neutral grey comparison series (prior period, "everything else") must be keyed `other`: the one key exempt from the series guard's chroma floor; it still needs the ΔE separation from its neighbours.
3. Compose the chart with marks from the allowed `@tanstack/charts/*` subpaths, and make it answer to focus (the geometry seam): `useChartFocus(items, { announce })` is one handle; pass it as `focus` to `CartesianChart`, `Heatmap` or a segmented `Meter` (the host owns the one tab stop, arrow keys and polite announcement), dim the rest against `plot.focused`. The figure's `value` and `delta` are the answer and stay while a hover moves (`basalt/primitive-bypass` refuses `focus.readout` there; owner review 2 saw n04's headline swap on every hour); the focused datum's reading goes in the figure's `period` and `status`, the readout line whose box keeps its width, through `focus.readout(rest, read)` (the rest value again on leave), or in the tooltip, never a placeholder delta (n20 put a placeholder 'partial' delta on its figure). A held run (a table row that stands for a window or a span of items) is read whole through `focus.span`, never through `focus.readout`, which reads its first item: `focus.span.length ? readRun(focus.span) : restStatus`, where `readRun` prints the run's best or its sum, as the table row does (n57 .7 named the first night of a held run, n62 .55 the first hour of a window whose row held mean and peak). Build the check into the hover frame: hold a list row and read the figure against the row. `plot.plotHeight` is the plot, `plot.data` every raw row; `fillOpacity` is one number per mark, so split rows into one mark per opacity. Name the marks that matter, a few: on a band axis `x.ticks` as a list of the keys that matter (a number only thins), elsewhere a `text` mark placed by `chartLabels` (n13 printed four of 18 payees and named none of the top risers). The mark that answers carries the answer at rest: the leading line's end value, the totals on the top bars, the gap at today (n22, n24, n25, n26 and n11 scored F2 with the answer in the hero only). A bar series takes `values: true` (or `(v, d) => text`) and the kit labels each bar, a stack by its total, and drops labels by priority where room runs out, never by hand with `chartLabels` (n28, n31). Cap the domain where one value crushes the rest (`y.domain`, a stack included; a `Sparkline` takes `cap`; the kit draws an off-scale caret and the tooltip and readout keep the true value), say how many a saturated ranking hides, and give a zero series a visible mark: a zero bar keeps a baseline tick that takes focus (n24, n26, n23, n22, n25). The accent goes to the item that answers (the culprit hop, the leading signal): a bar series' `highlight: (d) => boolean` paints those bars in the accent and keeps their labels last to drop, never a second series split off for colour (n28). One fact takes one status hue, never a red pill beside an amber dot (n25, n26, n11). A `Sparkline` with `axisLabels` is a chart host: give each point its name or day, `{ value, label: 'W41' }` or `{ value, at }`, so the stepped readout and its announcement say which point it is; a bare number reads only its place, `#8` (n27, /cards). A hover already pins x and y on the axes of every cartesian chart: never hand-draw a crosshair or an axis readout. Marks drawn through the seam set `legend: false` on their series and name them in `ChartCard` `legend`, whose entries toggle and isolate the series with the same `key` (c12 had a static legend). Name each line at its own end with a `text` mark, not in the legend only, and set `spread: 'x'` or `'y'` on a dot series whose dots coincide (n51, n52, n56). Items the question names sit on the chart's axis (stalled books on the time axis, hops in path order), and an ordinal such as a difficulty is a mark, not a word in a column (n11, n25). A page renders `focus.live` only where no chart takes the focus; a chart renders it itself. Name axes with `y.title` / `xTitle`; a vertical rule is `xRefLines`, a log x `xScale: 'log'`; place direct labels with `chartLabels.place` at `plot.x`/`plot.y` pixels, and a region the question asks about is a zone with `axis: 'x'`, never a hand-drawn `rect`: [custom-marks](references/custom-marks.md).

```tsx
const focus = useChartFocus(stages, { announce: (d) => `${d.name}: ${fmt(d.behind)} behind best` })
;<ChartCard title="Pace" figure={{ value: fmt(total), delta: restDelta,
  status: focus.readout(restStatus, statusOf), period: focus.readout('whole run', (d) => `${d.name}: ${fmt(d.behind)}`) }}>
  <CartesianChart label="Pace" data={stages} x={(d) => d.at} series={series} y={{ title: 'Seconds behind' }} focus={focus}
    marks={(plot) => {
      const rows = plot.rows('behind')
      const rest = rows.filter((r) => plot.focused !== null && r.datum !== plot.focused)
      return [dot(rest, { x: 'x', y: 'y', fill: SERIES.pace, fillOpacity: 0.25 }),
        dot(rows.filter((r) => !rest.includes(r)), { x: 'x', y: 'y', fill: SERIES.pace })]
    }} />
</ChartCard>
// A form the page draws itself (recipe `seam-bar`, or `seam-track` for marks on a time axis): the container is the tab stop, each mark an item; the kit dims the rest.
<div {...focus.target} aria-label="Context window" className="flex">
  {parts.map((p, i) => <div key={p.key} {...focus.item(i)} style={{ flexGrow: p.tokens }} />)}
  {focus.live}</div>
```

Linked parts: every list of the page's items (a `DataTable`, a `BarList`, a timeline) joins the one focus, with or without a chart on the page (n23 drew a `BarList` and a table of the same monitors and linked neither; n33 and n36, pages of tables, wired no focus and lost 2 each). Draw the chart at the list's grain, one bar per ticket grouped by status zones, so `DataTable focus` joins the two by id (n37 drew bars per status beside tickets and linked nothing). One `useChartFocus` goes to the chart host as `focus`, to `DataTable focus` and `BarList focus`, or is spread on each row through `focus.item(i)`, so pointing at or stepping to an item in either focuses it in the other; the figure's readout names the focused item, and a row with no entry in the focus clears it. With no chart the page renders `focus.live`. The announcement names the focused item's values in the tooltip's order, for the series the datum draws only: a day row that one series fills announces that series and never a series with no point there (n58 .18 announced "Batch pool 2 %" on an undrawn step), and every readout value carries its unit through the series `format` (n58 .15); the announcement names the focused item's values in the tooltip's order, never a verdict alone (`${name}: ${a}, ${b}, ${c}`; n22, n25 and n23 announced "stable"). When the period follows focus the tooltip is off (`tooltip={false}`): a pointer opens one readout, never three (n24, n26). The list's own `onPointerLeave` clears, so the gap between rows never flashes the unfocused state, and the kit dims: the page sets no opacity. Stacked panes on one x each take the one `focus`, and a chart of a parent's children joins through `focus.hold` (n63 .8, n65 .26). A list of groups beside samples spreads `focus.groupItem(name)` (recipe `seam-linked`); the wiring is in [linked-parts](references/linked-parts.md).

4. Wrap it in `ChartCard`; pass `state={{ pending, error, empty }}` from the query. A `cursorValue` series colours its CURSOR dot per datum with `getMarker: (d) => ({ color })`; always-on markers are `Lines`-only, so a consumer-`marks` chart draws its persistent dots itself.
5. Run the repo's own gate (e.g. `make check`), which runs lint and typecheck; fix every finding at its source.
6. Open your own 1800 shot and run two checks first:
   - **Fact count, run, and one view, as a table.** After the first typecheck, run the count over the page source ([copy](../basalt-design/references/copy.md) § the fact count: it reads viewer strings and never a property name inside `${…}`, and done is an empty print: option keys never print, a printed word, phrase, number with its unit or `=constant` is cut from all but one string, never called an identifier or silenced by rewording code; report the first and last run, then list each measure with every word the page uses for it, since two words for one quantity keep one), then read the rest shot aloud for one fact in several places, and write one row per part at 1800x1200, `part | question | x,y w×h | joins`, in `docs/design/<screen>.md` or beside the page source ([basalt-design SKILL](../basalt-design/SKILL.md) step 6; cycle 11 lost X1 on 6 of 6 pages with a fact table written by hand). Before the loading shot run `rg -nU '(footer|sub|rows|delta|status)=\{\s*[\w.!]+\s+(\?|&&)' <page dir> -g '*.tsx'`: each hit is a slot that vanishes in loading and is changed to the same labels with empty values (n45, n47). A part-to-whole of a few parts is a one-row stacked `BarList` (`series` and `parts`, the series file's colours, legend rows spreading `focus.item(i)` or `focus.seriesItem(key)`, n29), and a `Heatmap` uses `annotate`, `states` and `tickCols` (n30): [basalt-design charts](../basalt-design/references/charts.md).
   - Then render the page and open every frame: rest, each fixture, loading, empty and error, each in light and dark at 1800 and 390, plus one keyboard frame after two arrow presses (`basalt-ui shot <route> --states keys` shoots it and names a page error; a frame not opened is a frame not checked; c10, c11, c13 and n02 opened a subset). Run the owner's self-check in the basalt-design `references/anti-defaults.md` first (count the accent series, no bare border, a hover sweep against the rest frame, the redraw test on marks you drew, a table view for a grid of record cards; the reviewer fails a component on each of the first four, Craft 4, 8, 9 and 10), then its device checklist on each: the fact count (list each fact the page states and where; a fact in two places is cut to one), one measure, one view, linked parts, console anatomy and calm interaction, answer annotated on the mark, baseline or limit drawn, status hue spent only on a state that needs action, label collisions and light-mode contrast, a focus mark beside the ring, tabular numerals and motion-safe transitions, the extreme true and a zero cue, binned bars with a unit caption, a time window drawn by the hour up to 7 days (basalt-design `references/charts.md`), the answer above the fold at 1800x1200 and at 390. Tick the keyboard frame as its own line: `basalt-ui shot <route> --states keys` shot, two arrows read, no page error (a crash on a key reads as a `KEY` line with the error, and the page is not done). 0 of 6 builders shot it in cycle 5 and 0 of 6 in cycle 6.
