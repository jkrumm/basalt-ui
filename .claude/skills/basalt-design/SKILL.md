name: basalt-design
description: Build or restyle a page in a basalt-ui app: name the question, pick the form, compose the shipped parts, then look at the page at desktop and phone width before calling it done.
when_to_use: The user asks for a dashboard, list, detail or settings page, or says a UI looks inconsistent or generic.
---

Method only. The law is `.claude/rules/basalt-*.md` and `./DESIGN.md`; props live in the generated docs (`node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/<name>.md`) and the shipped `.d.ts`. This file restates neither.

## Steps

1. **Question first** ([concept](references/concept.md)). Before code, write three lines: who opens this page, the one question it answers, the figures that answer it. A page that cannot name its question is a list of boxes. State the answer at rest as one hero figure that is the answer, not a count of things.
2. **Surface second** ([concept](references/concept.md)). After the question and before any form, write the parts the surface needs (the mark, the figure, the detail list or table), where each sits and what they share. A chart with a detail list sits beside it at a wide container, the list scrolling in its own region at the chart's height, and stacks at a narrow one; parts that show the same items share one focus, a table and a bar list included, with or without a chart on the page (a page of tables takes one `useChartFocus` over its rows), a chart drawn at the list's grain (one bar per ticket, not per status) so `DataTable focus` joins them by id, and the parts that answer one question sit beside each other ([charts](references/charts.md)); a page of rows still leads with a card whose figure, context line and footer answer the question ([cards](references/cards.md)); the footprint fits the question, so a comparison of two numbers is not a full-page card.
3. **Form third** ([encoding](references/encoding.md)). Pick each form from the data's shape with the question-to-form table in the AGENTS block. A figure that fits no shipped form is reported to the user, never faked with a hand-built box. Model output (chat parts, generated text, a block the model chose) takes the renderer and trust level in [ai](references/ai.md). Open the doc of every part you use before you write its props: [stat-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-card.md), [stat-group](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-group.md), [chart-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/chart-card.md), [cartesian-chart](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/cartesian-chart.md), [bar-list](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/bar-list.md), [data-table](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/data-table.md) ([table](references/table.md)), [section](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/section.md).
4. **Marks fourth** ([charts](references/charts.md), [cards](references/cards.md)). Compose with the parts and theme tokens; the rules guard the colour, spacing and import limits. A card follows the console anatomy ([cards](references/cards.md)). Every headline stands against a base the data offers, as a signed delta that names it ([cards](references/cards.md)); delta badges are tinted and carry their polarity and period. The accent goes to the item that answers, and one fact takes one status hue. Captions are sentence-case sans. Neutral by default; the single accent is spent where a decision turns on it. The primary action is ink-inverted, one per view.
5. **States and copy** ([states](references/states.md), [copy](references/copy.md)). Every part that loads data takes its phase from the query ([query-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/query-state.md)); empty states say why and what to do ([empty-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/empty-state.md)). Copy states facts: precise nouns with their scope, a live number in the summary line, and marks carry the change where a sentence would repeat them.
6. **Look before you stop.** Open your own 1800 shot and run two checks first, each its own line:
   - **Word economy, as a count.** Write one line per fact, `fact: every place it stands`; a fact with two places is cut to one, as cycle 8 did not (a direction sentence on all eight cards, the top Issue in the hero, the row and the title, the contract three times per card, a legend repeating its card's title) ([copy](references/copy.md)).
   - **One view.** No empty third of the page, no dead band under a short card, no table wider than its columns, no stack of bands that share no focus; two parts on one focus share one legend and one caption ([charts](references/charts.md)).
   - **The answer on the mark.** The mark that answers carries its value or name, and a part-to-whole of a few parts is a one-row stacked `BarList` with legend rows that spread the focus ([charts](references/charts.md)). At rest the rows that answer carry a verdict word, the failing items are named, the window that never clears and the at-risk tickets are marked, and the rule that makes an item late is stated once on its column or card.
   - **Age, target, anatomy.** An age or a duration is a `c.meter` scaled to the oldest, never bare text ([table](references/table.md)); progress against a target is a [Meter](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/meter.md) with zones or done against pace, never a rule over bars it does not measure, and the hero stands against the reference the data holds ([charts](references/charts.md)); a table is a `DataPanel` or a card with a title and a context line, mono only for ids and times ([table](references/table.md)); kinds take the series file's pairs, HTML marks through `seriesTokens` or `groupTokens` from `basalt-ui/tokens` ([charts](references/charts.md)); at 390 a chart keeps a plot as tall as its axis and each item keeps one line of detail.
   - **The owner's self-check.** Count the accent series, find every bare border, sweep the hover against the rest frame, run the redraw test on marks you drew and give a grid of record cards a table view ([anti-defaults](references/anti-defaults.md) § Owner self-check): the reviewer caps a build that fails the first four under the pass line.

   Then build and typecheck, render the page and open every frame: rest, each fixture, loading, empty and error, each in light and dark at 1800 and 390, plus one keyboard frame after two arrow presses (`basalt-ui shot <route> --states keys`, which names a page error). A frame not opened is a frame not checked: builders that opened a subset shipped the defects in the rest. Run the device checklist in `references/anti-defaults.md` against each: answer on the mark, baseline, one measure, colour budget, extremes and zeros, seam-drawn finish, fixture honesty, label collisions and light-mode contrast, the answer above the fold at 1800x1200 (full width for a working screen) and at 390, card anatomy, linked parts, one readout, console anatomy and calm interaction. Run the fact count before the last look and cut every repeat in the code ([copy](references/copy.md)); also check the colour (the common state neutral, a zero day no fault), one name per item, many small cards grouped by verdict, every phase on the same slots with a cause and a Retry, and the anatomy of each secondary card ([states](references/states.md), [cards](references/cards.md)). Tick the keyboard frame as its own line (`--states keys` shot, no page error): 0 of 6 builders shot it in two cycles. Every figure from the brief is on the page, nothing is cut, every control has a name. Fix at the source, rebuild, look again. Run the repo gate before reporting done.
