# n68: galactic core months

## The three lines

- Who and what: an astrophotographer plans the year. Which months give usable dark hours from this site, and how many hours does the ridge cost? Decision: which months to book.
- Answers: 380 h usable behind the ridge, 293 h ridge cost against the flat horizon (the pill), 7 of 12 months usable, labelled Mar to Sep on the zone; Feb and Oct never clear (footer, and the 0/8 and 0/12 labels).

## Color: meaning

- brand accent: usable hours behind the ridge (the bars, the legend swatch). Only series in the accent.
- pale ink step: ridge cost (the lost part of each stack).
- dark ink step: the ridge's highest line; mid ink step: its mean line.
- zone tint (ink alpha): months in which the core clears the ridge, a neutral band, not an accent.
- no status hue: nothing here needs action.
