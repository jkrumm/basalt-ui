## 1. Decision summary

1. **The ceiling holds and now has slack: 18 own lint ids, 16 used.** 15 consumer ids + 1 repo-only; `important-modifier` folded into `tw-off-law`, the unbuilt `compound-structure` reservation released (E5). A wider census than digest row 4 finds **60** proposed names, not 31 (section 3). Disposition: 27 forms of a contract id, 5 bought, 12 types or absent props, 7 dev assertions, gates or `doctor`, 9 skill text, deferred or consumer. DPO 15, DEFAULT-PENDING-OWNER; alternative: lift the ceiling and split `primitive-bypass`, `data-primitive`.
2. **A ceiling on ids alone is gameable.** Merging forms costs nothing on the counter. Second gated number: **58 consumer-visible forms** (E2; 59 until `router-search/view-transition` was removed, 2026-11-11). A **form is one fix message plus one fixture file**; the count is generated from the fixture list, so alternation inside one regex cannot hide growth and one fixture cannot stand for two different fixes.
3. **A rule survives only with a recorded defect or reproduced trap, a syntactic detector and a satisfiable fix.** This also binds admitted forms (section 3). Numeric container tiers and `max-md:` are forms, not skill text: they keep the viewport-keyed twin alive and tokens-visual and gap-token booked them. Literal `useSearch({ from })` stays a skill line. Types first: a prop that does not exist needs no fixture.
4. **Own 900 lines, buy the rest.** `@shadcn/lint` 0.2.5 + stylelint 18.0.0 + one `classPatternRule` factory replace ~7,700 authored lines (gap-guardrail). `oxlint-tailwindcss` stays out: its 61 console findings are a subset of shadcn's 66.
5. **Every message names the fix and the next rung of the bending ladder.** Diagnostics beat rule text: 1.00 vs 1.25-1.57 rounds, vendor-run on 7/8 vs 8/8 tasks (ext-guard), so it is a pattern to copy, not a measured effect size. A message saying only "not allowed" fails the table test. This serves argo's D 47 and T 42 buckets.
6. **No grace ladder, no warn level.** The preset is a committed, generated file; tightening arrives as a visible diff after a fleet probe (0 new findings). Argo paid 109 rewrites to a moving scale, so the scale freezes per major.
7. **Managed copies stay untouched by construction first, detection second.** Un-ejected primitives cannot drift (contract 4.1); generated files are regenerated and byte-compared; a weakened config is proven by a seeded offender per root, not by reading config keys. Edit-time denial is a convenience; commit and CI are the gate.
8. **Zones shrink to three, and K is dropped.** No definer exemption: contract 7.3 says app composites are "fully linted". I8's hole ("one local function switches three rules off") cannot recur if the exemption does not exist.
9. **The guarantee is a floor** (contract 0.5). Lint enforces "app code carries no colour class", fills and text alike, from a list generated from `table.ts` (tokens-visual 3.5; falsifier S4 re-allows text first); whether a colour earned its place stays with closed `tone`, required `polarity`, no `color` prop and the reviewer.
10. **`app-spacing` ships narrower than contract 9 wrote it** (E4): margin and `space-*` denied now; the padding form is built but off, because argo's own sites fail the satisfiable-fix test. F5P passed on a value rubric (1.0%) and cannot overturn that structural test, so it stays off until a structural retest; F5 REFUTED, the zone A allowlist does not flip (`spikes/allow-padding.md`, 2026-11-11).
11. **Six contract exceptions, section 15.** E1 is load-bearing: `.basalt/manifest.json` needs a digest per managed agent file, or invariant I24 dies.

**`doctor` weakened-guard checks.** Key-reading alone misses the cheap bypasses (argo's real config has `ignorePatterns`, `plugins`, per-glob `overrides`), so the proof is behavioural: (1) a seeded offender written into each declared root and linted through `basalt-ui lint`, removed in a `finally`, must fire: this catches a dropped `extends`, emptied `jsPlugins`, a widened ignore or `.oxlintignore` in one check; (2) `.oxlintrc.json` `extends` names the generated preset; (3) no managed id in `rules` or `overrides[*].rules`; (4) the installed oxlint equals the preset pin (shutterflow 1.83.0, argo `^1.91.0` fail this until bumped); (5) `.basalt/budget.json` and the eject count do not exceed `git show <default-remote-branch>:.basalt/budget.json`, so a raise needs a reviewed diff; no base ref prints skipped, never passed. `--print-config` was tried for (1): on 1.90.0 it shows merged native rules and `ignorePatterns` but no `basalt/*` from an extended JS plugin (F, argo), so it cannot replace the seeded offender.

## 8. Waivers and the edit-time loop

- **One dialect:** `// oxlint-disable-next-line basalt/x -- <why>`, `/* stylelint-disable-next-line x -- <why> */`. Codemod for the sites (63 by the digest; shutterflow measured 37, not 39). Native: unknown id waives nothing, dead waiver reported (F, gap-guardrail). A directive for a rule configured `off` is reported unused (S4 PASS 2026-11-11, `spikes/lint-edges.md`), but only with `--report-unused-disable-directives`, which `basalt-ui lint` passes; `waiver-needs-reason` is a plain plugin rule over `getAllComments()`.
- **Hook shape (DPO 15, DEFAULT-PENDING-OWNER; alternative: consumer wires the hook).** PostToolUse on `Edit|Write|MultiEdit` runs `basalt-ui lint --hook post-edit` through the managed shim (the path arrives on stdin), 0.27-0.5 s, exit 2 with stderr. Stop runs `lint --changed` once, bound per turn by `stop_hook_active` with the session counter as a ceiling (hook-sync, `spikes/hook-sync.md`: the hook side passes, S2 whether the model acts on exit 2 is pending an owner run; if it fails, Stop goes). PreToolUse is deny-only for section 7 paths. `init` registers through the local bin, never `bunx` (I25). **Fingerprint filtering is not built until S8 shows PostToolUse beats Stop alone** (S8 NOT RUN, pending an owner run, `spikes/lint-edges.md`; DPO 15 hook shape stays undecided); the efficacy evidence is a vendor README and vendor evals.
- **Hooks are advisory and Claude-only.** Findings can be ignored, Stop blocks once, Codex or OpenCode sessions get no edit-time signal. Rungs 10-11 never own a "never" (contract 9). Commit and CI are the gate; 6 of 12 repos run `sync --check` today, which is why `basalt()` carries the one drift check that must reach deploy builds and why section 9 makes wiring a `doctor` failure.
