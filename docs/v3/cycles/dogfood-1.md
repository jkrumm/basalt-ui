# Dogfood cycle 1

The factory console's overview slice rebuilt on master's published surface: the Flow page's stat row (four `StatCard`s with their trends and footers), one chart card (the Throughput page's `TimeChart` card, here the Issues in flight per day), the Issues as a `DataTable` with the console's project, origin and state filters, and the console's peek as a `PageAside`. The console has no single overview route (`/` redirects to Needs you), so the slice takes its regions from the two pages that hold them. The data is the console's own fixture shapes (`KIT_STATS`, `KIT_ISSUES`, `KIT_ORIGIN`, the Flow trend). A `?screen=` switch puts every frame in one phase for the shots. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle has not converged: 3 new P1, 7 new P2 and 6 P3, beside 5 known findings (2 P1, 3 P2) that earlier cycles filed and that this slice hit again. The StatCard holds: its shell, inset, figure row, delta pill and footer match the console's anatomy step for step in both schemes, and its phases keep the card's height. What fails is around it. The stat row leaves a third of the row empty at 1800 px (dogfood-1.1). A table filtered by the page's controls says `No rows yet` and offers no way back (dogfood-1.2). A title with a reason truncates to ten characters while other columns hold spare width (dogfood-1.3). The app wrote about 42 lines the kit could own and 1 escape hatch. To land, the PR also carries two package commits that this cycle's brief forbids: the route-budget exclusion an earlier cycle filed and that has not shipped yet (dogfood-1.20), and six quality-matrix waivers for the kit findings the matrix reports on this route (dogfood-1.21).
