# Dogfood cycle 2

The factory console's overview slice rebuilt a second time on master's published surface, after cycle 1's six fix rows and its four known rows shipped: the Flow page's stat row (four `StatCard`s), one chart card (the Throughput page's `TimeChart` card, here the Issues in flight per day), the Issues as a `DataTable` with the console's project, origin and state filters, and the console's peek as the table's detail shown in a `PageAside`. The build is new; the data is cycle 1's fixtures in the console's shapes, imported as they are. A `?screen=` switch puts every frame in one phase for the shots. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle has not converged: 1 new P1, 4 new P2 and 6 P3, beside 2 known P2 that earlier cycles filed. Cycle 1's three P1 are gone: the stat row fills its four tracks, a filtered-out table says so and offers Clear filters, and a title keeps its words while its reason gives way. Of cycle 1's 18 findings, 13 are fixed, 1 is partly fixed and 4 are open P3s. What fails now is the deep link: `?issue=login-crash` opens the aside on #642, then the table clears `issue` from the URL while the aside still shows the Issue, and no row is marked (dogfood-2.2). A reload or a copied link after that shows nothing. The app wrote about 18 lines the kit could own (cycle 1: ~42) and no escape hatch (cycle 1: 1).
