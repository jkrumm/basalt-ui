# Dogfood cycle 3

The factory console's overview slice rebuilt a third time on master's published surface, after cycle 2's five fix rows and its two known rows shipped. The console moved since cycle 2: its Flow page dropped the board and gained its own chart card ("Issues over time", with a Count and a Window choice) and a sortable Issues table. This build follows it: the stat row (four `StatCard`s with their definitions), the Issues over time card as a v3 `ChartCard` with two segmented widget controls, the Issues as a `DataTable` filtered by the page's project, origin and state fields, and the console's peek as the table's detail in a `PageAside`. The stat and Issue data are cycle 1's fixtures in the console's shapes; the trend is new, in the shape of the console's `trendLines`. A `?screen=` switch puts every frame in one phase for the shots. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle has not converged: 1 new P1, 3 new P2 and 6 P3, no known findings. Cycle 2's P1 is fixed: a deep link keeps `?issue=login-crash` and the row carries `aria-current`. But no row is visibly marked, deep-linked or clicked, in either scheme: the table's CSS paints the selected row with `--vx-surface-row-selected`, a 2.x variable the v3 sheet never emits, so the background stays transparent (dogfood-3.2). Cycles 1 and 2 checked the attribute and never the pixels. The P2s: a card's own choice needs a second store and a layout route (3.1), short text columns keep their width while the title's reason is cut (3.3), and a filtered-out table drops the page's sentence for one that names a search the table does not have (3.5). The app wrote about 15 lines the kit could own (cycle 2: ~18) and no escape hatch (cycle 2: 0).
