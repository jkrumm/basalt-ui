# Dogfood cycle 4

The factory console's overview slice rebuilt a fourth time on master's published surface, after cycle 3's three fix rows shipped. The console moved again since cycle 3: Flow became the Overview at `/` (`/flow` redirects there), and an open Issue now sits under one of five work words (running, paused, ready, held, checking) instead of the old six states. The stat row is Running, Paused, Not started and Shipped this week, the chart card's Count choice has four options, and the list opens on a word or on what closed lately. This build follows it: a counts sentence, the stat row (four `StatCard`s with their definitions), the Issues over time card as a v3 `ChartCard` with two segmented widget controls, the Issues as a `DataTable` filtered by the page's project, origin and state fields, and the console's peek as the table's detail in a `PageAside`. The Issues are cycle 1's fixtures moved onto the five words; the stats and the trend are in the shapes of `statsOf` and `trendLines`. A `?screen=` switch puts every frame in one phase for the shots. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle has not converged: 0 P1, 4 new P2 and 7 P3, no known findings. Every P1 and P2 of cycle 3 is fixed: the open row is tinted in both schemes, short text columns give their width to the title, the card's Count and Window live on the page's one store, and a filtered-out table says the page's sentence. The new P2s: a widget id another page holds blanks the whole app at load (dogfood-4.1), a four-option segmented group breaks its labels (4.2, cycle 3's P3 grown), a status column cuts `Closed recently` with no ellipsis (4.4), and a page summary stays busy in an error phase (4.5). The app wrote no line of glue the kit could own (cycle 3: ~15) and no escape hatch (cycle 3: 0).
