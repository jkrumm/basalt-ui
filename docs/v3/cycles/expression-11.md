The draft (`findings-draft.md`) was written by a subagent from the reviews, the builder reports and the shipped text; the lead checked its classes against the builder wish lists and kept them.

Three classing calls rest on evidence beyond the reviews:

- The joined-row reveal (.8, .28, .38) is `primitive`: the shipped charts reference promises that a chart focus brings a joined row into view, and the three builds use the documented wiring (`DataTable focus` with a fill height).
- The half-width table (.7, .16, .27, .48, .59) is `primitive`: five of six builders put the table beside the chart first, saw it turn into phone cards at about 750 px, and moved it full width, where its first column stretches.
