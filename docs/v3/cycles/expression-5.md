Six one-shot trials on master after the cycle 4 fix rows landed, all native cases with no reference. They are scored against `docs/v3/expression/rubric.md`. Protocol: `docs/v3/EXPRESSION.md`.

One case passed in round 0: n14 at 82. The round-0 median is 72.5 and the minimum 52. On the F and K items alone, the trend measure against cycles 1 and 2, the median is 75 and the minimum 60. Most of the rest is label placement that cannot see zone labels, rule labels or bars, and line charts that do not dim on focus.
