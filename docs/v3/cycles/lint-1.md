# Lint cycle 1

The shipped rules measured from a consumer's side: every rule of the generated rule table, the bought shadcn rules the preset turns on, the stylelint preset, the token check `basalt-ui lint` runs, doctor's weakened-config check and check-theme. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`; brief: `docs/v3/issues/cycles/v3-cycle-lint-1.json`.

The cycle has not converged: 7 P1, 12 P2 and 6 P3. The rules catch what their own fixtures write: 106 of 138 seeded phrasings for the 15 table rules, all 23 stylelint phrasings and both token phrasings. The misses are the second spelling an agent reaches for: a hoisted fallback, `navigate` beside `Link`, `react-markdown` beside `streamdown`, overflow in a stylesheet. The false alarms are worse than the misses. On the playground the app preset reports 89 findings from the table rules, and 47 of them are false: the series file the colour rule sends you to (14), `m-0` resets (22), the colour of a mark drawn through the seam the bypass rule names, and focus rings (7). Three wiring defects turn the guard against the app that follows it: an ejected primitive lints red, a shipped recipe lints red, and a bare `/* oxlint-disable */` silences every rule while doctor reports nothing. None of this showed in the gate, because the playground and the repo still lint with the 2.x plugin.
