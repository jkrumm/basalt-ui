The cycle has not converged: 0 P1, 8 P2 and 4 P3. Every cycle 6 fix holds: each lint-6 miss its row turned into a form is caught, its four probes are reported, and the three examples/react controls of lint-6.3 are no longer read as cards. The older seeds are caught at 853 of 913 (cycle 6: 807); the 60 left are the misses earlier cycles judged, two of them now valid by design (a `50%` radius, lint-6.11) or rerouted (`@custom-media` reported by the at-rule list, lint-6.15). The playground reports 0 findings at `app` and `parts`, the 36 ejected primitives report 0, and all 102 valid-code marks are silent, 27 of them new beside the shapes the cycle 6 fixes widened.

The new false alarms come from a rule no cycle has measured: `no-use-before-define`, which `v3-expr-5-keys` turned on at `app` and `parts` after an expression build threw on a closure that read a `const` declared below it. It cannot tell that crash from a closure called later, so it fires on the lazy router read `routerDefaults`' own doc page writes (lint-7.1). The init template and the playground carry three reasoned waivers for it, and none of them counts: the waiver budget and doctor's weakened-config check know only the `basalt/*` and `shadcn/*` ids, so switching the core rule off in `.oxlintrc.json` passes doctor (W18, the first weakened-config probe missed in four cycles, lint-7.2). The other P2s are this cycle's 160 phrasings, caught at 110: two dark spellings that surface-recipe reads with a fix that keeps the fork, Tailwind's `--color-*` theme variables read and re-pointed in a module, a fourth ring of six packages, `res.body?.getReader()`, `setInterval(refetch, …)`, and an orientation query or a user-agent sniff in JS.
