# Owner review 1: the rerun

The owner marked down seven expression pages in his first hands-on review on 2026-11-18 (`docs/v3/expression/calibration-owner-2.md`) and asked for the causes to be fixed, not the pages. Seven rows fixed the kit, the lint, the skill and the rubric. This rerun tests those fixes the only way that counts: a fresh builder per case, who never saw his remarks, the old build or the rubric, built each page again in one shot on the fixed kit.

Seven of his eight remarks are met on the rebuilt pages, and one is partly met. n35 now draws its week with the shipped `IntervalTrack` and no hand marks, but its event titles are cut to "Sprint…" and "Desig…" at 1800, and at 390 only three of about two dozen are named. Four remarks were met by the kit fix alone: the old builds, shot again on today's kit, already toggle and isolate the c12 legend, draw quiet row rules on n02 and n04, and keep n04's plot still under the pointer. The other four (n15's colour, n27's table, n32's chain, n35's track) needed the rebuild: the fixed kit plus the fixed skill led a fresh builder to the part the owner asked for.

On the recalibrated rubric (with the R items and the rendering cap) round 0 has a median of 79 and a minimum of 74. Three of seven pass at 80 or more (n15 91, n35 85, n27 83). In their own trials the same cases had a first-try median of 70 under the older, softer rubric.

## What the lead changed outside the builds

- **The old n35 model.** The `/kit/interval-track` demo imported the old n35 build's calendar fixture (`scenarios/expr/n35/model.ts`). It now reads the same file from `apps/playground/src/demo/calendar-week.ts`, so the case directory could start empty.
- **Old series groups.** `HEALTH_SERIES`, `RIDE_SERIES` and `WALK_SERIES` in `demo/series.ts` belonged to the old n27, n04 and n15 builds only, and their comments described the old builds' colour choices. They were removed before the builders started; n04 and n15 appended new groups that reuse two of the names. Every other group is untouched.
- **One fixture string.** n32's fixture titled an Issue "Drop the Mantine bridge from the shell", which the repo's legacy-names gate refuses; the lead reworded it to "legacy bridge". It is data, not the build's form, and no frame depends on it.
