# Owner review 2: the rerun

In his second hands-on review on 2026-11-19 the owner named three pages: expr-n04 ("still has that confusing flickering when hovering the chart, fix the cause not the symptom"), the strip of figures at the top of the shell-5 Fleet overview ("a way less thought-through version of cards, way too simplistic, boring and not structured enough") and Fleet settings ("forms need way more love", "way richer and more beautiful appearance and options and rendering"). Rows `v3-owner-2-chart-calm`, `v3-owner-2-stat-strip` and `v3-owner-2-forms-grammar` changed the kit, the lint and the skill. This rerun tests those fixes the way owner review 1 did: one fresh builder per page, who never saw his remarks, the old build or the rubric, built it again in one shot on the fixed kit.

One remark is met and two are partly met. n04 no longer flickers: its headline is the answer and never changes on hover, and the extended hover guard, which fails both old builds, passes the rebuild at 1800 and 390 on every fixture. The overview's first row now carries a comparison or a state in every cell, but it is still the strip, without the icon and trend the stat-row verdict designed, so it reads as the same band with chips. Fleet settings uses the new form grammar and reads as designed (titled sections, label-left settings rows, a save footer, radios, a stepper, checkboxes), but it is sparse rather than rich: three short sections, options with no description line, and the Members and Danger zone sections of the old build gone.

## What the lead changed outside the builds

- **The old n04.** Its directory was emptied to a placeholder (its source is kept in `src-rold/n04`) and its `RIDE_SERIES` group, used by nothing else, was removed before the builder started; the builder appended a new `RIDE_SERIES`.
- **Lint and guard in full for the rebuilt pages.** The trial builds keep `figure-readout` off and the hover guard skips their card until their rerun (`PLAYGROUND_FORM_WAIVERS`, `TRIAL_PAGES`). For n04 and shell-6 both now run in full: `PLAYGROUND_REBUILT_TRIALS` in `packages/basalt-ui/scripts/gen-oxlint.ts` adds an override after the waiver with no form off (mirrored in `apps/playground/.oxlintrc.json`), and `REBUILT_PAGES` in `hover-shift.ts` lets the matrix check their card. Without it the lint and the guard would pass both pages trivially. These are the only edits under `packages/basalt-ui/`, which the brief fences off: the playground's lint config and the matrix test, not the kit.
- **The shell case.** Owner review 1 rebuilt expression cases, which have a case text in `cases.md`. The shell case has none of its own; the builder got the shell-5 brief's case paragraph, the fleet domain the earlier cycles built (six services, 90 days of deploys, the team's settings) and the stand-in server's behaviour (a slow first load, a failing call, a missing id), so the before and after are the same app. The lead's probe of the shell exercises (crumb, palette, keyboard walks) was not repeated: the builder ran its own and the quality matrix covers the routes; this rerun judges the three remarks.
