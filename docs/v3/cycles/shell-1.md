# Shell cycle 1

A three-page app on master's published surface: a fleet overview (a `PageBar` with a title, two actions and one filter, a `StatGroup` row and a `ChartCard`), a services page (a `SplitPage` of services beside the chosen one's deploys, and a `PageAside` inspector opened from a deploy row) and a settings page (an `InnerNav` in a `SplitPage`). One `defineNav` section feeds the rail, the phone More sheet, the breadcrumb and the document title. Then the brief's exercises, run in Chrome against the playground build: the phone (390x844), the docked aside (1800x1200), the collapsed rail, the route error, pending and missing states, and a keyboard-only pass. Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle has not converged: 6 P1, 7 P2 and 6 P3. The frame itself holds: the docked aside takes its 360 px beside a 1200 px main and keeps it with the rail collapsed, the collapsed rail persists across pages, the skip link lands in main, the More sheet lists the three pages, Back walks every pushed field, and the keyboard pass found no trap. The P1s are where a page wires the kit's parts together. `InnerNav` bound to a `defineControls` push field draws anchors with no `href` (shell-1.3). A table row cannot show its detail in the page's aside through any published path (shell-1.1). On a phone the default settings section cannot be opened (shell-1.5) and a row's inspector does not open (shell-1.8). The route error's Try again does not retry (shell-1.7). Mod+K never reaches the palette that lists the pages (shell-1.9). The app wrote about 80 lines the kit could own, 10 of them layout.
