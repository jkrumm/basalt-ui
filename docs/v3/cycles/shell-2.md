# Shell cycle 2

The fleet app of shell cycle 1, rebuilt on master once every fix row had shipped:

- a fleet overview: a `PageBar` with a title, two actions and one filter, a `StatGroup` row and a `ChartCard`;
- a services page: a `SplitPage` of services beside the chosen one's deploys, and a `PageAside` inspector opened from a deploy row;
- a settings page: an `InnerNav` in a `SplitPage`.

One `defineNav` section feeds the rail, the phone More sheet, the breadcrumb, the document title and the palette. The brief's exercises then ran in Chrome against the playground build:

- the phone at 390x844;
- the docked aside at 1800x1200;
- the collapsed rail;
- the route error, pending and missing states;
- a keyboard-only pass.

Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

Every `cycle-shell-1:` workaround is gone. Cycle 1 had:

- 12 workaround comments;
- about 80 lines of glue;
- 7 casts;
- 2 places that knew a shell internal.

This build has:

- 1 workaround comment;
- 3 lines of layout glue and 1 line of other glue;
- no cast;
- no place that knew a shell internal.

Every cycle 1 P1 holds:

- `InnerNav` takes a push field as it is;
- a row's detail lands in the page's aside, docked on a desktop and a sheet on a phone, deep link included;
- the default settings section opens on a phone;
- Try again re-runs the loader;
- Mod+K reaches the one palette that lists the pages.

The pending screen shows. The rail and the crumb move together. The missing record names itself and offers a way back. The phone header keeps its title.

The cycle has not converged: 1 P1, 1 P2 and 8 P3.

- **The P1 (shell-2.1):** a selection's crumb parent, the documented way up from a `SplitPage` selection, has no working form:
  - a path string is drawn as a bare anchor that reloads the whole document and drops the page's other params;
  - a `NavTarget`, which the type accepts, is drawn as unlinked text.
- **The P2 (shell-2.2):** focus. On a phone, the `SplitPage` back link and an `InnerNav` choice drop focus to `<body>`.

The P3s are focus placement, the route screens' missing heading, the phone crumb rule, one filter's three steps and the phone stat strip.
