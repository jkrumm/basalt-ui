# Shell cycle 3

The fleet app of shell cycle 2, rebuilt on master once both fix rows (`v3-shell-2-crumb`, `v3-shell-2-shell`) had shipped:

- a fleet overview: a `PageBar` with a title, two actions and one filter, a `StatGroup` row and a `ChartCard`;
- a services page: a `SplitPage` of services beside the chosen one's deploys, and a `PageAside` inspector opened from a deploy row;
- a settings page: an `InnerNav` in a `SplitPage`.

One `defineNav` section feeds the rail, the phone More sheet, the breadcrumb, the document title and the palette. The brief's exercises then ran in Chrome against the playground build:

- the phone at 390x844;
- the docked aside at 1800x1200;
- the collapsed rail;
- the route error, pending and missing states;
- a selection's crumb parent;
- a keyboard-only pass.

Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

The cycle 2 workaround is gone. The crumb parent is now the documented `NavTarget`, and the per-route screen spread is gone with `route-screens.tsx` and the layout route. Cycle 2 had:

- 1 workaround comment;
- 3 lines of layout glue and 1 line of other glue;
- 3 focus losses and 3 misplaced focuses.

This build has:

- no workaround comment;
- the same 3 lines of layout glue and no other glue;
- no cast;
- no place that knew a shell internal;
- 1 focus loss, at both widths.

Every cycle 2 finding holds as fixed:

- a crumb parent is an in-app link that keeps the document and the retained params (shell-2.1);
- a phone `SplitPage` swap puts focus in the shown pane, both ways (shell-2.2);
- Try again lands focus in main (shell-2.3);
- closing the aside returns focus to the latest opener (shell-2.4);
- a palette or More-sheet jump lands focus in main (shell-2.5);
- the phone keeps the page's parent and drops the section, as `app-shell.md` now says (shell-2.6);
- the route screens carry one `h1` (shell-2.7);
- the playground installs the route screens once (shell-2.8);
- the lone range filter is an inline select, two interactions (shell-2.9);
- the phone stat strip shows two stats per row (shell-2.10).

The cycle has not converged: 1 P1, 2 P2 and 1 P3.

- **The P1 (shell-3.1):** the docked aside shows a record the URL no longer names. Taking the crumb parent or the rail link to Services clears `deploy`, yet the aside stays docked on "Deploy v3.13.0". Picking another service keeps Checkout's deploy open beside Billing's table.
- **The P2s (shell-3.2, shell-3.3):** the crumb parent carries `aria-current="page"`, so a screen reader hears the way up announced as the current page. A click on it drops focus to `<body>`.
- **The P3 (shell-3.4):** the palette lists two destinations with the same label without telling them apart.
