# Shell cycle 4

The fleet app of shell cycle 3, rebuilt on master once both fix rows (`v3-shell-3-aside`, `v3-shell-3-shell`) had shipped:

- a fleet overview: a `PageBar` with a title, two actions and one filter, a `StatGroup` row and a `ChartCard`;
- a services page: a `SplitPage` of services beside the chosen one's deploys, and a `PageAside` inspector opened from a deploy row;
- a settings page: an `InnerNav` in a `SplitPage`.

One `defineNav` section feeds the rail, the phone More sheet, the breadcrumb, the document title and the palette. The brief's exercises then ran in Chrome against the playground build:

- the phone at 390x844;
- the docked aside at 1800x1200;
- the collapsed rail;
- the route error, pending and missing states;
- a selection's crumb parent, with a record open;
- a switch of the selection with a record open;
- a keyboard-only pass.

Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

Cycle 3 left no workaround to delete: its build had no `cycle-shell-3:` comment, and this one has none either. Every cycle 3 finding holds as fixed:

- the crumb parent, the rail link to Services and a switch of the service each close the docked aside (shell-3.1);
- the crumb parent carries no `aria-current` (shell-3.2);
- a click on it hands focus to main, at 1800 and at 390 (shell-3.3);
- palette entries of one label name their section (shell-3.4).

The cycle has not converged: 1 P1, no P2 and 1 P3.

- **The P1 (shell-4.1):** a deep link to a record leaves an empty sheet in the document, stuck half closed. At 1800, Tab past the aside's Close panel enters it. The sheet slides over the docked aside, and Tab, Shift+Tab and Escape all keep focus on its Close button: a keyboard trap. At 390 the same sheet sits below the viewport, and Tab after More lands on its invisible Close. Cycle 3's route has it too (`inspect2.log` I6), so cycle 3's "no keyboard trap" was a miss: its keyboard pass walked a record opened by a click, which leaves no such sheet.
- **The P3 (shell-4.2):** a palette jump to a page whose loader is still pending leaves focus on `<body>` until the page arrives, about 650 ms on the first services load.
