# Shell cycle 5

The fleet app of shell cycle 4, rebuilt on master once both fix rows (`v3-shell-4-sheet`, `v3-shell-4-shell`) had shipped:

- a fleet overview: a `PageBar` with a title, two actions and one filter, a `StatGroup` row and a `ChartCard`;
- a services page: a `SplitPage` of services beside the chosen one's deploys, and a `PageAside` inspector opened from a deploy row;
- a settings page: an `InnerNav` in a `SplitPage`.

One `defineNav` section feeds the rail, the phone More sheet, the breadcrumb, the document title and the palette. The brief's exercises then ran in Chrome against the playground build:

- the phone at 390x844;
- the docked aside at 1800x1200;
- the collapsed rail;
- the route error, pending and missing states;
- a selection's crumb parent, with a record open;
- a switch of the selection with a record open, by click and by keyboard;
- a palette jump, and a More-sheet jump, to a page whose loader is pending;
- a keyboard-only pass through every page, walking a record reached by a click and by a deep link, at 1800 and at 390.

Protocol: `docs/v3/CYCLES.md`.

Cycle 4 left no workaround to delete: its build had no `cycle-shell-4:` comment, and this one has none either. Both cycle 4 findings hold as fixed:

- **shell-4.1 (P1):** a deep-linked record leaves no sheet of the table's own in the document, at 1800 or at 390 (`inspect.log` I1-dialogs-before, I3; `inspect2.log` I7, I9, I10). At 1800, Tab past the aside's Close panel reaches the document end (`walks.log` W2, `inspect.log` I1), as it does for a record opened by a click (W1). The walk from the top of a deep-linked page ends at the document end after 81 stops (`keyboard.log` K2, `walks.log` W3). Cycle 4's own route, built from the same master, is clean too (`inspect2.log` I6).
- **shell-4.2 (P3):** a palette jump to Services on its first load puts focus in main at about 205 ms, while the outgoing page still shows, and main keeps it when Services arrives at about 730 ms (`inspect.log` I5, by click and by Enter). A More-sheet jump on the phone does the same (`walks.log` W6).

**The cycle converges: no P1, no P2, one P3.** It files no fix row and no cycle 6.

- **The P3 (shell-5.1):** a Tab pressed while a jump's page is pending moves focus out of main to `<body>`, where it stays until the page arrives about 430 ms later; main then takes focus back. Pressed in the 100 ms before the pending screen shows, the Tab enters the outgoing page's Range select first, and focus falls to `<body>` with it.
