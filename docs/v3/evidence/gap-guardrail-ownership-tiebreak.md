## 0. Verdict

1. **F** Correction: `ext-shadcn-state.md` finding 4 ("no lint package exists, the guarantee column is empty, that is basalt's niche") is outdated. `github.com/shadcn-ui/lint` (MIT, created 2026-09-02, 3,341 stars, 8 releases 0.1.3 to 0.2.5 in 9 weeks, 85 of ~100 commits by shadcn, 9 open issues; `gh api`) ships an agent-first linter that I ran. **J** The niche moves: shadcn guards _generic_ class hygiene and primitive restyling; basalt's lasting guarantees are the _laws_ it cannot express (homes, container-over-viewport, boundaries, waiver accountability, computed palette, geometry).
2. **F** Buy-vs-own validated on 94 console files, 45 rollhook files, 5 rb files, 35 astro files. Buy `@shadcn/lint` (engine) and own its contracts+messages as generated data. **Do not** put `oxlint-tailwindcss` in the preset: on the console `shadcn/no-arbitrary-values` flags 66 and `tailwindcss/no-arbitrary-value` 61, a strict subset (61 of 61 shared, 0 only-otw); its one unique rule (`no-restricted-classes`) is a 25-line regex that I reproduced as basalt rules and which was stricter (stacked variants).
3. **F** Two real holes found in the bought layer: `@shadcn/lint` never flags `bg-black/40`, `text-white`, `border-white/10` (hard-coded exemption, `dist/index.js:4724` `FIXED = new Set(["white","black"])`), also under a closed `@theme`, where they silently emit nothing; and `p-[13px]` is "fixed" to `p-3.25`, a fractional step basalt's spacing law would reject. Both need own rules/messages.
4. **F** Target: **15 shipped basalt plugin ids + 1 repo-local** (vs 23 + 4 today, plus 27 regex kinds) + 6 bought `@shadcn/lint` rules + 5 stylelint core rules + 2 computed CLI checks. Regex engine, grace ladder, retired ids and `audit-allows.ts` all go.
5. **F** Edit-time: PostToolUse on the edited file + one Stop re-check; PreToolUse only for protected paths. Single-file lint costs 0.27 s (own+shadcn) to 0.5 s (with oxlint-tailwindcss).

### (b) Grace ladder after 3.0: drop it

- **F** The ledgers are empty since 2.26.0 (`PLUGIN_RULE_GRACE = {}` `oxlint-plugin.js:4347`, `GRACE_PERIOD_KINDS = {}` `index.ts:907`), yet `check-grace.ts` (144), `grace.test.ts`, 14 retired ids, and `// Ships:` comment assertions are maintained. The stated justification is "majors are banned here, so a consumer has no semver channel" (`MAINTAINING.md:385`).
- **J** Premise gone for 3.0, and even if a no-majors line resumes the ladder is the wrong tool: its real value was the expiry test (five kinds sat at `warn` five minors). Three cheaper channels replace it: (1) the preset is a **committed, generated file** (`sync` writes it, `sync --check` is red until applied, I24): tightening arrives as a visible diff on the consumer's pull, not as a silent bump through `extends` from `node_modules`; (2) a **fleet probe** before a rule enters the preset at `error`: run it over the `consumers.json` repos on the mini, 0 new findings or it waits for the next generation; (3) **exact pins** of bought plugins in that file (patches change findings, F release notes).
- **Policy sentence replacing C16:** v3 ships no design rule at `warn`. A rule is `error` or it is skill prose. (Advisory-forever warns are what the ledger existed to chase.) Because I4 ("new form = new id because severity is per id") only existed for the ladder, related forms merge into one id (`viewport-keyed`, `primitive-bypass`).
- Keep one lesson: a dropped/renamed export or rule needs a MIGRATING row (rollhook on 0.4.2 for 19 minors, I27).
