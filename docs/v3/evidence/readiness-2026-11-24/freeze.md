# basalt-ui v3: status of the STOCKTAKE freeze list

Audit 2026-11-24, master at `2fed04240` (2026-11-23). Read-only: nothing was built, tested or run.
Source: `docs/v3/STOCKTAKE.md` (2026-11-21, master `360110f1b`). Paths are repo-relative; `P` = `packages/basalt-ui`.

## Verdict

The freeze pass has not started. 768 commits landed since 2026-11-21, and they went into feature growth: mail,
schedule, flow canvas, board, assistant, appearance accents, panels, the Accordee-grade shell. 117 of the subjects
are budget raises. Of the 13 freeze rows only row 13 is closed (`994acc36b`). The install-path blind spot of
section 3 is exactly as it was. What did land matches section 7: the loop is paused (`169186658`), the design and charts
skills are rewritten to 8 KB (`0a936b0f2`) and the matrix record is slimmed (`b706e21c3`). The AGENTS-block
factory line was already gone before the stock-take was written (`f6daf2af6`, 2026-11-20). The design that section 2 says holds
still holds. Every new feature since then, though, adds names that the unfinished naming pass (row 7) will also have to cover, so
the freeze gets more expensive with each commit.

## What is sound (verified at HEAD)

- One package; the required-peer gate and `surface-gates.ts` walk the built graph per subpath (`P/scripts/surface-gates.ts:128`).
- Typed nav where it is guarded: `defineNav` refuses an unregistered router (`P/src/router/nav/types.ts:185-186`); `NavTarget` is opaque with a WeakMap (`P/src/router/nav-target.ts:20-41`).
- Budget schema parsing is strict and named (`P/src/cli/budget/index.ts:49-60`); raises leave an audit row (`:143`).
- The agent layer has byte and line gates per file plus a whole-layer total (`P/scripts/check-coverage.ts:72-83`); the shipped consumer block is fleet-neutral (`P/agent/templates/AGENTS-block.md.tpl:36`).
- The expression loop is stopped and its history is out of the skills (`169186658`, `0a936b0f2`).

## Proposed order

1. Freeze wave A (deletions and renames, one migration row each): rows 3, 4 (after the playground move), 5, 6, 11, plus `surfaces.ts` and root `AGENTS.md`.
2. Trial app from the tarball through `init --profile app` in CI; move the playground to `basalt()`; delete `basaltViteConfig`.
