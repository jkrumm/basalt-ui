# Owner calibration 2

The owner reviewed the v3 playground by hand on 2026-11-18, each case as its builder left it after its last round, on the kit at master `7c454b3a0`. It was his second calibration of the expression rubric, after cycle 2's (`docs/v3/cycles/expression-2.md` § Owner calibration), which added the X items. His overall verdict: "generally we are on the right path and we are going good; some inconsistencies in spacing; I don't like this topbar you often used". On the cases he named he disagreed with the reviewers: the highest round-0 score of cycle 9 was the build he called poorly designed, and a build that passed first try was the one he called screaming blue. This page records his words, the scores those cases got, what the rubric missed, and the rescore under the items that close the gap (`docs/v3/expression/rubric.md` § Rendering).

Fresh reviewers (Sonnet, the cycles' reviewer tier, one per case, none of which had seen the case or any earlier score) scored the four cases from their last-round shots and source snapshots under the new rubric (`.evidence/v3-shots/owner-2-rescore/` in the main checkout, gitignored: the prompts, which are each case's last-round prompt with the rubric path, the R rows and the cap added, and the reviews).

| n04  | `expression-4/r2` | 72        | 79                 | 79          | R1 0: every row of the windows list has a bare `border-b`, black in light and white in dark                 | yes          |

**n15 needed a second pass.** The first rescore scored F4 2 and passed it at 83: it read the card titles, which name each measure, as the "second meaning" the item then allowed for a second series in the accent. That is the loophole the owner's remark is about, so F4 now says a title naming each measure is no second meaning, and that a series drawn all in the accent where the question points at one of its marks scores 2 at most; Craft 4 of the shipped rubric took the same sentence. A second fresh reviewer, on the tightened text, scored F4 1 ("minutes bars (all 7) plus the pace line, in one blue across the two cards") and the case 78. The two reviewers also differ by five points on the items that did not change (X1, X5, K5), the usual spread between two reviewers of one build; the cap is what keeps F4 1 under the line whatever that spread does to the sum.

**n32 fails by its sum, not by the cap.** It drops from 81 to 79 on R2 2 (a key row 61 px tall against 41 px, a section title 16 px off the table's x) and R3 2 (the 390 list frame sits 24 px above the rest frame, which the reviewer could not tie to the page or the harness). His remark is mostly about form, a chain drawn as an indented table, and F1 already scores it 2; the chain view is `v3-owner-1-chain-view`'s. The margin is one point, so a reviewer who scored R3 3 would pass it at 81: the rubric names the defect class, and the form point stays where it was.

**n04's jump is not in its frames.** The reviewer found the plot at one position in every `--focus--` and `--sweep-gap--` frame, so R3 scored 3, and n04 fails on R1 alone. The jump the owner saw loops: a period line shown only on hover pushes the plot down below the `regular` container size, the pointer leaves the plot, focus clears and the line collapses (`docs/v3/issues/owner-1/v3-owner-1-chart-hover.json`). A still frame 60 ms after the pointer arrives can catch either half, and the cycle 4 shots ran on an older kit than the one he saw. R3 now counts a move in a logged plot box along the sweep as a shift, and the hover-shift guard of `v3-owner-1-chart-hover` is the check that sees it: the next cycle's lead tool should log the plot box at each sweep step (a signal filed from Issue 2284, folded into Issue 2134).

## Rerun rescore: kit-drawn titles (2026-11-19)

The owner-review-1 rerun (`docs/v3/cycles/owner-review-1.md` § Finding 6) showed R2 read as an excuse for kit-drawn defects: n35's reviewer scored R2 3 on a track with nine ellipsised titles because `IntervalTrack` drew them and charged them to F6, while n32's scored R2 1 for a `ChainView` cut at its panel edge. The owner judges the surface, not who drew it. R2 now counts a clipped, cut or ellipsised label or title the same whether the app or a kit part drew it, and the default 3 for a page of shipped parts is gone; F6 keeps the clipped values, and Craft 10 of the shipped rubric says the same.

Two fresh reviewers (Sonnet, none of which had seen either case) scored the rerun's round-0 shots and source snapshots (`owner-review-1/r0`, `src-r0`) on the new text (`.evidence/v3-shots/owner-1-rerun-rubric/`, gitignored):
