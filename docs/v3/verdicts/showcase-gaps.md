# Showcase gaps

Built with the playground's Kit gallery (`apps/playground/src/routes/kit/`, nav group Kit), from owner review 1 on
2026-11-18. Each row is a place where the shipped surface could not do what a Kit page needed. None is built around:
the page leaves the part missing (or shows the nearest shipped form) and this list names it. Nothing here is filed;
the list is for the owner. One capability of the brief is not in the gallery at all: resizable panes (the first
row), because the shipped part fails the quality matrix and neither a restyle nor a waiver may route around it.

## Forms

Built with the Forms group of the Kit gallery (`apps/playground/src/routes/kit/forms/`, nav group Kit: Forms), from
owner review 2 on 2026-11-19: inputs in their states, choices, dates and numbers, buttons, and three whole forms bound
to TanStack Form (`@tanstack/react-form`) through FormField's `FieldLike` port. Six shipped parts fail the quality
matrix at rest, so the gallery pages hold them back and say so on the page, as the resizable row above did; the whole
forms still use Combobox, the language chips and NumberField where the job needs them, inside the create dialog and
the settings tab, which the matrix does not open. Each row lands with v3-owner-2-forms-grammar unless it names another
home.

`docs/v3/verdicts/forms-research.md`. Closed means the part ships and the gallery uses it; carried means it stays open,
with the reason.
