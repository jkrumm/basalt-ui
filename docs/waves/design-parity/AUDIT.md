# Two-way audit — basalt against the Keel reference (Wave 30)

Wave 30, step 1 of `docs/waves/PLAN.md` Part 3. Every pattern in [keel/inventory.json](keel/inventory.json)
is scored in **both directions**. Keel is a look-only reference: this page describes what the crops show in
basalt's own words and quotes none of Keel's copy, markup or styles. The decisions that follow from these
scores live in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md); this page is the evidence.

- **Inputs:** the Wave 29 crops (`.design-parity/keel/patterns/`, `.design-parity/w29/basalt/`, gitignored),
  [BENCHMARK.md](BENCHMARK.md), [w29/compare.json](w29/compare.json) and the fleet survey in
  [SPEC.md § Fleet needs](SPEC.md#fleet-needs). The scorer was a fresh agent that did not write the code. The
  lead checked the sharpest claims against the source (the delta fill, the stale `StatCard` JSDoc, the Badge
  default) and re-graded three verdicts (nav-item, loading-state, side-panel-card).
- **Verdicts:** _basalt ahead (keep)_, _on par_, _learn from the reference_, _missing_.
- **Root-cause classes:** `token/scale`, `anatomy` (a component's structure), `missing component`,
  `chart-in-card` (chrome of a chart inside a card), `recipe` (basalt can already express it; nothing tells an
  agent to).
- **Caveat on the evidence:** four basalt stand-ins are loose matches. side-panel-card maps to a buttons demo
  card, empty-state to the table's no-results state, status-pill to Mantine's default solid Badge, and
  form-field to a label-left `FormRow`. Where the stand-in is the wrong job, the verdict says so.

The headline is not spacing. **basalt can draw most of the encodings it lacks** (`Sparkline`, `Donut`,
`BandStrip`, `Heatmap`, `Bars`) but its cards put a number in a box, its table cells are all text, and its
defaults show neither. Most gaps are `recipe` or `anatomy`, then a short list of `missing component`s that
the fleet actually hand-rolls.

## Cross-cutting

1. **Expression is the gap.** The reference gives each metric the encoding its data has: a ring for a
   bounded ratio, a segmented bar with a target tick, a trend with an end dot, weekday bars for rhythm, a tick
   meter for a score. basalt has the chart engine for most of these; what is missing is two marks (a meter, a
   ranked bar list), the card and cell slots that host them, and the guidance that picks them.
2. **The default card is half empty.** A `StatCard` with no `sparkline` renders a figure floating in the top
   half. This is the single largest reason basalt cards look poor, and it is a default and a recipe, not a
   missing component.
3. **The delta contradicts the law.** basalt's own law says a delta is text colour, never a fill; the
   component paints a 13 % tint. Fix the code, not the law.
4. **Scales:** card inset 11/13 sits off any grid; card radius 7 against control 6 gives nested surfaces a
   1 px step; 32 px controls wrap the table toolbar; the 16 px gutter makes an expanded layout feel packed
   rather than dense. Each is a decision in [SPEC.md § Scales](SPEC.md#scales).
5. **Type:** rows run 2–3 px larger than the reference's (body 15 against 13), which is what wraps dense
   rows on compact. The hero figure at 24/600 mono shouts. Mono itself is identity and stays.
6. **Accent leaks:** the subtle button label and (to verify) the tab underline spend the accent on chrome.

## Where basalt is ahead — protect

- **Charts:** one host on TanStack Charts, the `CartesianChart` rung, kinds, scales, tooltips and the
  engine-boundary guard. The reference's charts are small hand-drawn glyphs with no axes, tooltips or scales.
- **Derived theming:** HCT palette, contrast guaranteed in both schemes, `--vx-on-*` labels, `check-theme`.
- **Tables:** the same 40/36 heights, mono tabular figures, typed columns, the `meta.priority` fold,
  `renderCard`, virtualisation, column-shaped skeletons.
- **`StatCard` internals:** container-query figure, never-truncated value, unit slot, `breakdown`, measured
  sparkline slot, `query` branch.
- **Responsive law:** size class, container class and pointer tier as three axes; a real compact bottom bar.
- **Type identity:** three faces (body, head at 88 % stretch, mono for numerals). Do not flatten to one sans.
- **Guards and budgets:** types, lint rules, budgets and drift checks. The reference has no equivalent.
