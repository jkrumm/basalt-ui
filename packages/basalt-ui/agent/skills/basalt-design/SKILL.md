Method only. The law is `.claude/rules/basalt-*.md` and `./DESIGN.md`; props live in the generated docs (`node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/<name>.md`) and the shipped `.d.ts`. This file restates neither.

## Steps

1. **Question first** ([concept](references/concept.md)). Before code, write three lines: who opens this page, the one question it answers, the figures that answer it. A page that cannot name its question is a list of boxes. State the answer at rest as one hero figure that is the answer, not a count of things.
2. **Surface second** ([concept](references/concept.md)). After the question and before any form, write the parts the surface needs (the mark, the figure, the detail list or table), where each sits and what they share. A chart with a detail list sits beside it at a wide container, the list scrolling in its own region at the chart's height, and stacks at a narrow one; parts that show the same items share one focus ([charts](references/charts.md)); the footprint fits the question, so a comparison of two numbers is not a full-page card.
3. **Form third** ([encoding](references/encoding.md)). Pick each form from the data's shape with the question-to-form table in the AGENTS block. A figure that fits no shipped form is reported to the user, never faked with a hand-built box. Open the doc of every part you use before you write its props: [stat-card](docs/stat-card.md), [stat-group](docs/stat-group.md), [chart-card](docs/chart-card.md), [cartesian-chart](docs/cartesian-chart.md), [bar-list](docs/bar-list.md), [data-table](docs/data-table.md), [section](docs/section.md).
4. **Marks fourth** ([charts](references/charts.md), [cards](references/cards.md)). Compose with the parts and theme tokens; the rules guard the colour, spacing and import limits. A card follows the console anatomy ([cards](references/cards.md)). Delta badges are tinted and carry their polarity and period. Captions are sentence-case sans. Neutral by default; the single accent is spent where a decision turns on it. The primary action is ink-inverted, one per view.
5. **States and copy** ([states](references/states.md), [copy](references/copy.md)). Every part that loads data takes its phase from the query ([query-state](docs/query-state.md)); empty states say why and what to do ([empty-state](docs/empty-state.md)). Copy states facts: precise nouns with their scope, a live number in the summary line, and marks carry the change where a sentence would repeat them.
6. **Look before you stop.** Build and typecheck, then render the page and run the device checklist in `references/anti-defaults.md` against it at 1800 and 390 wide, light and dark, and one keyboard-focused frame: answer on the mark, baseline, colour budget, seam-drawn finish, fixture honesty, label collisions and light-mode contrast, the answer above the fold at 390, card anatomy. Every figure from the brief is on the page, nothing is cut, every control has a name. Fix at the source, rebuild, look again. Run the repo gate before reporting done.
