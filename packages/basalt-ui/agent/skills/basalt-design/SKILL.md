---
name: basalt-design
description: Build or restyle a page in a basalt-ui app: name the question, pick the form, compose the shipped parts, then look at the page at desktop and phone width before calling it done.
when_to_use: The user asks for a dashboard, list, detail or settings page, or says a UI looks inconsistent or generic.
---

Method only. The law is `.claude/rules/basalt-*.md` and `./DESIGN.md`; props live in the generated docs (`node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/<name>.md`) and the shipped `.d.ts`. This file restates neither.

## Steps

1. **Question first** ([concept](references/concept.md)). Before code, write three lines: who opens this page, the one question it answers, the figures that answer it. A page that cannot name its question is a list of boxes. State the answer at rest as one hero figure that is the answer, not a count of things.
2. **Surface second** ([concept](references/concept.md)). After the question and before any form, write the parts the surface needs (the mark, the figure, the detail list or table), where each sits and what they share. A chart with a detail list sits beside it at a wide container, the list scrolling in its own region at the chart's height, and stacks at a narrow one; a form laid out along time or a chain (`IntervalTrack`, `ChainView`, a timeline) takes the row's full width with its list below it, since a half-width card is for a form that reads at half width (n35's week track and n32's chain each lost half their width beside the list: cut titles, a cut last column); parts that show the same items share one focus, a table and a bar list included, with or without a chart on the page (a page of tables takes one `useChartFocus` over its rows), a chart drawn at the list's grain (one bar per ticket, not per status) so `DataTable focus` joins them by id, and the parts that answer one question sit beside each other ([charts](references/charts.md)); a page of rows still leads with a card whose figure, context line and footer answer the question ([cards](references/cards.md)); the footprint fits the question, so a comparison of two numbers is not a full-page card.
3. **Form third** ([encoding](references/encoding.md)). Pick each form from the data's shape with the question-to-form table in the AGENTS block. A figure that fits no shipped form is reported to the user, never faked with a hand-built box. Model output (chat parts, generated text, a block the model chose) takes the renderer and trust level in [ai](references/ai.md). Open the doc of every part you use before you write its props: [stat-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-card.md), [stat-group](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-group.md), [chart-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/chart-card.md), [cartesian-chart](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/cartesian-chart.md), [bar-list](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/bar-list.md), [row-list](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/row-list.md), [data-table](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/data-table.md) ([table](references/table.md)), [section](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/section.md).
4. **Marks fourth** ([charts](references/charts.md), [cards](references/cards.md)). Compose with the parts and theme tokens; the rules guard the colour, spacing and import limits. A card follows the console anatomy ([cards](references/cards.md)). Every headline stands against a base the data offers, as a signed delta that names it ([cards](references/cards.md)); delta badges are tinted and carry their polarity and period. The accent goes to the item that answers, and one fact takes one status hue. Captions are sentence-case sans. Neutral by default; the single accent is spent where a decision turns on it. The primary action is ink-inverted, one per view. Anything that enters, leaves or moves is timed from the motion scale and a preset ([motion](references/motion.md)), never a number.
5. **States and copy** ([states](references/states.md), [copy](references/copy.md)). Every part that loads data takes its phase from the query ([query-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/query-state.md)); empty states say why and what to do ([empty-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/empty-state.md)). Copy states facts: precise nouns with their scope, a live number in the summary line, and marks carry the change where a sentence would repeat them.
6. **Look before you stop.** Run the word count over your page source and fill the one-view table from your 1800x1200 shot, before the last build and not from memory; a page without both is not done. Cycle 10 named both checks as lines and lost X1 and X2 on 6 of 6 pages; cycle 11 asked for a table of facts and lost X1 on 6 of 6 again, because a list the builder writes holds what the builder already said once. A command prints the repeats, so the count is run, not written.
   - **Fact count.** After the first typecheck and before the shot, run the word count of [copy](references/copy.md) § the fact count over the page's own strings: it reads the words a viewer reads, never a property name inside `${…}`, and prints every word said in four or more strings, every phrase said in two or more (title, subtitle, unit, axis title, legend, footer, chip, empty sentence), a number with its unit or label (`[3 h]`, `[low #]`) and a text constant read in two places (`=unit`). Done is an empty print: a printed item is cut from all but one string, never called an identifier and never silenced by destructuring or rewording the code, and an option key (`header`, `label`) is never in it, so a print that names one means the command was altered. Then list each measure and every word the page uses for it: one quantity under two words ("usable" and "clear") keeps one ([copy](references/copy.md)). Report the first and the last run. An empty print is a floor, not the check: it reads literal strings, and cycle 15's six builders each ended at one and lost X1 on all six. The read-aloud section below is the check.
   - **Read aloud (a required section).** After the count prints nothing, read the 1800 rest shot aloud, part by part from the top, and write a `## Read aloud` section in the design file: one line per fact (a measure, a name, a time, a state, a limit) as `fact | every slot that says it`. A fact on two lines keeps one slot, one measure with two numbers is a defect, the empty sentence stands on one card, and a sort and a window are facts. Four repeats are invisible to the count and each is a repeat: a value one helper prints into several parts (`stamp()` in a strip sub, a column and a cell: `rg -n '<helper>\(' <page dir>`, each caller is a slot); a label map entry shown in a status, a footer, a cell and a subtitle (`rg -n '<MAP>' <page dir>`, each use is a slot); a state said by a zone caption, a pill and a sub line; a legend entry that repeats an axis title or the card title. A page with no section, or one whose lines name one slot each without a grep of its helpers and maps, is not done ([copy](references/copy.md) § Worked: the read-aloud pass).
   - **One view.** One row per part, in `docs/design/<screen>.md` or, where the repo fences writes, a file beside the page source: `part | question it answers | x,y w×h | what it joins` (a focus, a figure, a legend). A table beside its chart stays a table only while its priority 1 and 2 columns sum to 480 px (`ch` × 8 + 24 each): add the sum to its row ([charts](references/charts.md) § One view). Fail on each: two parts that answer one question in two rows; a part full width for four rows or one mark; a gap as wide as a card in the first 1200 px; a part that joins nothing and has no word for why it stays; a part that restates another part's measures (fold it into the chart's rows or drop it); a card large for six bars; an empty lower half. Change the surface, not the spacing, and shoot again ([charts](references/charts.md) § One view).
   - **Four lines from cycle 11.** A loading frame passes every slot the ready frame draws, the footer included, with the labels held and the values empty ([states](references/states.md)). A question with two clauses has a number at rest for each, in the figure, a status or a footer fact ([cards](references/cards.md)). At 390 the answer text wraps or moves to a list, never onto a mark label or a card `sub`, which the kit cuts to one line ([charts](references/charts.md)). One status hue and one accent meaning across the cards of a view, a table's status column included ([charts](references/charts.md)).
   - **Seven lines, each with its worked case.** The answer on the mark at rest, agreement between sources and the extreme items named, drawn from every source ([charts](references/charts.md)). A baseline for every figure, a delta that names its base, a duration or an age as a fact or a meter and never a delta pill ([cards](references/cards.md)). One meaning per colour: write `colour: meaning` for each hue ([charts](references/charts.md)). A context line on every card, a unit caption on count bars, mono for times and ids ([cards](references/cards.md)). Never clip your own scale under the data: a cap is the kit's clamp with its true value ([charts](references/charts.md)). One `cursorGroup` per readout that differs, and `focusItems.holds` by window, never by the exact minute ([charts](references/charts.md)). At 390 a strip cell fits one line and the column that answers stays in view ([table](references/table.md)).
   - **Eight lines from cycle 12.** The answer is a label on its mark, never only a hero or a `sub` line beside it, and each line is named at its own end ([charts](references/charts.md)). Every part's empty names its cause and next step, never the kit default or `empty={null}` beside a chart that states its cause ([states](references/states.md)). `mono` is an id or a timestamp, never a sentence, and a slot keeps one face at rest and on focus ([table](references/table.md)). At 390 the answering rows come first, a chain takes the full width and a table that repeats the chart is dropped ([charts](references/charts.md)). Spans over time are an `IntervalTrack` and each measure the case names is drawn. An extreme fixture keeps the rest readable: a capped heat domain, thinned per-hour marks. A dense dot series sets `spread` ([charts](references/charts.md)).
   - **Nine lines from cycle 13.** One measure at two grains, or a second series, in the accent is two classes unless a word on the page names it ([charts](references/charts.md) § Colour budget). A readout of a held run reads `focus.span`, its best or its sum, never the first item ([basalt-charts](../basalt-charts/SKILL.md) step 3). Many near-identical series: the leader against a quiet rest, or a `c.spark` per row ([charts](references/charts.md)). Each part's empty names its real cause and next step, checked as a list ([states](references/states.md)). `mono` on every id and timestamp, hover stamps and `period` readouts included ([cards](references/cards.md)). An axis or zone ends at the last slot's end and no tick repeats the first ([charts](references/charts.md) § Honest marks). Charts that share an x share one card, and a card is sized to what it holds ([charts](references/charts.md) § One view). A meter column sets `card` ([table](references/table.md)); only drawn series announce and every readout carries its unit; a subtitle fits one line at 390; a loading frame keeps every slot, the subtitle included ([states](references/states.md)). A hero line that must name its rule is a `CartesianChart` with a labelled `refLines` rule, never a `Sparkline` ([charts](references/charts.md)).
   - **Lines from cycle 14.** The focused period and status carry the focused item's own number, and a figure kept on the whole period never sits beside a period tag naming one day or week ([charts](references/charts.md) § Linked parts). Stacked panes each take the one `focus`, and a chart of a parent's children joins through `focus.hold` ([charts](references/charts.md) § Linked parts). The accent is the pointed-at mark and the rest of its series stays in ink; a zone that answers carries its word and a dense series is binned; at 390 the second clause is in the first screen; a `highlight` fill gets a `LegendEntry`; a custom `text` mark sets `fontSize` and `fill` from tokens; a status on a span focus fits one line ([charts](references/charts.md)). A delta pill compares the figure's own window ([cards](references/cards.md)). `c.entity` takes `mono` for ids, and a table measure draws its size with `c.meter` or `c.spark` ([table](references/table.md)). Separate sources keep separate `retry` functions ([states](references/states.md)).
   - **Lines from cycle 15.** Size each part to its content: a `Heatmap` of few rows takes `height`, and `matchHeight` only in a card its rows fill; no `min-h` that outgrows the rows; `phone: 'columns'` for ten or more numeric rows at 390; footer `rows` on every strip cell so none leaves a band; no tall card beside a column of two ([charts](references/charts.md) § One view, [table](references/table.md)). A delta pill is `polarity: 'neutral'` unless its direction is the page's one status hue; a warn tone or dot beside a red and green pair is a second hue; two accent lines told apart by dash alone take direct labels ([charts](references/charts.md) § Colour budget). A part at a grain no focus arm reaches is folded into a part that joins, or dropped ([charts](references/charts.md) § Linked parts). At 390 every item of a band axis is named: `x.tickFormat`, and `formatCol` on a `Heatmap` ([charts](references/charts.md) § One tab stop). A delta stands against a baseline the data holds and shows it ([cards](references/cards.md)). A state a clause asks for is a word on its item, the noun a figure counts is its `unit`, every percent goes through `useFormat().percent`, a strip figure in an inset is `StatGroup variant="cards"`, and a `Heatmap` takes stable accessors and data ([charts](references/charts.md), [cards](references/cards.md)).
   - **Age, target, anatomy.** An age or a duration is a `c.meter` scaled to the oldest, never bare text ([table](references/table.md)); progress against a target is a [Meter](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/meter.md) with zones; a table is a `DataPanel` or a card with a title and a context line ([table](references/table.md)); kinds take the series file's pairs, HTML marks through `seriesTokens` or `groupTokens` from `basalt-ui/tokens`.
   - **The owner's self-check.** Count the accent series, find every bare border, sweep the hover against the rest frame, run the redraw test on marks you drew and give a grid of record cards a table view ([anti-defaults](references/anti-defaults.md) § Owner self-check): the reviewer fails a component on each of the first four (Craft 4, 8, 9 and 10).

   Then build and typecheck, render the page and open every frame: rest, each fixture, loading, empty and error, each in light and dark at 1800 and 390, plus one keyboard frame after two arrow presses (`basalt-ui shot <route> --states keys`, which names a page error). A frame not opened is a frame not checked. Run the device checklist in `references/anti-defaults.md` against each. Tick the keyboard frame as its own line (0 of 6 builders shot it in two cycles). Every figure from the brief is on the page, nothing is cut, every control has a name. Fix at the source, rebuild, look again. Run the repo gate before reporting done.
