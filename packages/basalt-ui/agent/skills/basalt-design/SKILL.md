Method only. The law is `.claude/rules/basalt-*.md` and `./DESIGN.md`; props live in the generated docs (`node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/<name>.md`) and the shipped `.d.ts`. This file restates neither.

## Steps

1. **Question first** ([concept](references/concept.md)). Before code, write three lines: who opens this page, the one question it answers, the figures that answer it. A page that cannot name its question is a list of boxes. State the answer at rest as one hero figure that is the answer, not a count of things.
2. **Surface second** ([concept](references/concept.md)). After the question and before any form, write the parts the surface needs (the mark, the figure, the detail list or table), where each sits and what they share. A chart with a detail list sits beside it at a wide container, the list scrolling in its own region at the chart's height, and stacks at a narrow one; parts that show the same items share one focus ([charts](references/charts.md)); the footprint fits the question, so a comparison of two numbers is not a full-page card.
3. **Form third** ([encoding](references/encoding.md)). Pick each form from the data's shape with the question-to-form table in the AGENTS block. A figure that fits no shipped form is reported to the user, never faked with a hand-built box. Model output (chat parts, generated text, a block the model chose) takes the renderer and trust level in [ai](references/ai.md). Open the doc of every part you use before you write its props: [stat-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-card.md), [stat-group](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/stat-group.md), [chart-card](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/chart-card.md), [cartesian-chart](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/cartesian-chart.md), [bar-list](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/bar-list.md), [data-table](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/data-table.md) ([table](references/table.md)), [section](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/section.md).
4. **Marks fourth** ([charts](references/charts.md), [cards](references/cards.md)). Compose with the parts and theme tokens; the rules guard the colour, spacing and import limits. A card follows the console anatomy ([cards](references/cards.md)). Delta badges are tinted and carry their polarity and period. Captions are sentence-case sans. Neutral by default; the single accent is spent where a decision turns on it. The primary action is ink-inverted, one per view.
5. **States and copy** ([states](references/states.md), [copy](references/copy.md)). Every part that loads data takes its phase from the query ([query-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/query-state.md)); empty states say why and what to do ([empty-state](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/empty-state.md)). Copy states facts: precise nouns with their scope, a live number in the summary line, and marks carry the change where a sentence would repeat them.
6. **Look before you stop.** Build and typecheck, then render the page and open every frame: rest, each fixture, loading, empty and error, each in light and dark at 1800 and 390, plus one keyboard frame after two arrow presses (`basalt-ui shot <route> --states keys`, which names a page error). A frame not opened is a frame not checked: builders that opened a subset shipped the defects in the rest. Run the device checklist in `references/anti-defaults.md` against each: answer on the mark, baseline, one measure, colour budget, seam-drawn finish, fixture honesty, label collisions and light-mode contrast, the answer above the fold at 390, card anatomy, the fact count, one view, linked parts, console anatomy and calm interaction. Write the fact count before the last look: each fact the page states, and where; a fact in two places is cut to one ([copy](references/copy.md)). Tick the keyboard frame as its own line (`--states keys` shot, no page error): 0 of 6 builders shot it in two cycles. Every figure from the brief is on the page, nothing is cut, every control has a name. Fix at the source, rebuild, look again. Run the repo gate before reporting done.
