# Chart labels

The answer is on the mark. The figure, the baseline and the label on the mark that answers state it once between them.

## A headline value has a baseline

- Every headline figure has a baseline: a zero line, a reference line, a zone, or a signed delta that names its period. `refLines` and `zones` are data on [CartesianChart](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/cartesian-chart.md). [type:ChartRefLine] [type:ChartZone]
- Against a limit, draw the limit and print the distance to it; a limit that exists only as a color rule becomes geometry or a signed distance.
- The threshold that defines the answer is a `refLines` rule or a zone on the chart as well as any footer value, and the mark that matters says why it matters, beyond its name ("over 4 kt apart").

## Label the mark that answers

- Label marks with `text` marks placed by [chartLabels](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/chart-labels.md): it measures, avoids overlaps and drops what is too wide. A kind such as [Bars](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/bars.md) draws only its own marks, so a word on one mark is a [CartesianChart](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/cartesian-chart.md) with a `text` mark from `@tanstack/charts/text`.
- The mark that answers carries the answer at rest: the leading line's end value, the total on each top bar, the gap at today. The hero or a footer is a second place at most, and a label the plot edge drops is placed again inside the plot.
- The hero states the answer with its verb ("Runs out 13:52") and at most one phrase of baseline; the one mark the page is about carries the annotation and the rest recede. [useChartFocus](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/use-chart-focus.md) holds the focused datum for the marks, the figure and the live region.
- Name each line and each rule at its own end (the series name, and the end value where it differs), so the legend is the second place. A [Sparkline](node_modules/basalt-ui/docs/sparkline.md) takes `axisLabels` and draws its `reference` bare, so a line that must name its rule is a CartesianChart with a labelled `refLines` rule.
- A state a clause of the question asks for ("waiting") is a word on its item through the bar series' `values: (v, d) => text`.
- Rows that answer carry their verdict word at rest; the failing items are named as well as counted, and a count of named things names them where they fit. A span that stays under the bar is annotated on its span.
- A footer fact that belongs to one mark is on that mark: a `highlight` or a per-bar `opacity` for a state, `values` for a number, a `refLines` rule on every pane for a median, a legend-only entry for a fill without a series.
- When sources agree or part, the agreement is on the mark; an extreme is drawn from every source as well as the median, and the items that cross the line are marked on their dots with `chartLabels.place`.
- A custom `text` mark sets `fontSize` and `fill` from tokens (`BASALT.text.micro`, `BASALT.ink`), or it draws at the body size in the body ink ([custom marks](../../basalt-charts/references/custom-marks.md)). [type:ChartPlot]

## Axes and ticks

- On a band axis `x.ticks` as a list of keys labels exactly those bands, the answering one first; a number only thins. Default thinning is for an ordered axis (time, a number), and a numeric tick that nothing on the page explains is a gap.
- At 390 every item of a band axis is named: `x.tickFormat` writes the short tick apart from the readout's `x.format`. Count the ticks in the 390 shot against the items.
- A band or zone that is the answer carries its words; a rule at 25, 50 and 75 is a zone with a `label`. A dense series is binned to the grain the question reads.
- `x.title` and `y.title` name the axes: a count axis and a share-of-limit axis carry one ("Requests", "% of limit"), and a `y.format` keeps a decimal for a domain under 2 units so every tick keeps its own label.

## One name

- An item has one name across the axis, the list and the hero. A name that is too long is cut by the kit's truncation; an invented short one splits the item in two.
